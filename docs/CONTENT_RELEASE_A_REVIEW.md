## Release-wide engagement gate

Before approval, every Release A passage must:

- establish a concrete question, goal, surprise, or conflict within its opening;
- give the reader something consequential to track or discover;
- use specific actions and choices rather than a lesson delivered as a speech;
- contain a turn, complication, or correction of an initial belief;
- end with a satisfying outcome that does not merely restate the moral;
- remain original and avoid imitating Reyana's favorite shows or franchises.
